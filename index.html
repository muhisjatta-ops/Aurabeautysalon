Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to wash my hair before braiding?

We offer "Hair Wash & Deep Conditioning" for D400. If you prefer to wash it yourself, please make sure it is completely detangled and product-free before your appointment.

Do I provide my own meche?

It is up to you! We have a Knotless Braid package for D1800 that includes 2 packets of premium meche. If you bring your own, the service is only D700.

How long do lash extensions last?

With proper care, our volume lashes can last 3-4 weeks. We recommend coming in for a refill every 2-3 weeks to keep them looking full.
